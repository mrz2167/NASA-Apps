File 00 · Briefing
EVERY GENERATION GETS ONE SKY.

Since 2025, a NASA telescope called SPHEREx has photographed the entire sky every six months, in 102 colours of light your eyes can't see.

Most of what it sees sits still. Some of it doesn't: asteroids, nearby stars, cold "failed stars" called brown dwarfs. Your job is to catch what moves, then find out what it's made of.
